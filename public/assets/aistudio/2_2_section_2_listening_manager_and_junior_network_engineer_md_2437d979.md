## 2.2.2 Listening - Exercise: Manager and Junior Network Engineer

### Transcript

**Bobby (Woman):** Hi Peter, I've recently taken a look at your latest performance and I wanted to discuss one of the issues that was handled by you. It's the one with unauthorized access points being connected to our internal network.

**Peter (Man):** Hi Bobby, yes I remember that one. It was an unusual one, wasn't it?

**Bobby:** Yes indeed, but it's not the technical aspect that brought it to my attention. I'd say it's rather the fact that you omitted some actions required.

**Peter:** Oh really? Can you tell me a little more about it? What didn't I do that I should have?

**Bobby:** The situation that you faced was in fact a security incident, and as such, it needed to be reported to our security team.

**Peter:** But I did report it to you, remember?

**Bobby:** Of course I do. Nevertheless, besides telling me, it does need to be reported to the security team so that they can start their own procedures. They are the best people to analyze it to see if this course of action can be reproduced, and if it can, then they'll come up with a corrective action plan. After looking at your case, it might be an issue for our whole network, so its impact can be very big. Such incidents can expose the security risks within our network, and they do need to be eliminated.

**Peter:** I see that now. In my previous job, there was no such policy, and I haven't got used to all the procedures here. I have to admit that they can be a bit confusing at times. I definitely do need to review the procedures again, don't I?

**Bobby:** Look, Peter, I might be sounding a little more serious than I mean to be. I noticed this just after you closed the ticket, so I believe it's not too late to report the incident to the security team. It does have to come from you, however. You haven't been working for us for so long, so these kinds of mistakes are acceptable at this point in time. That's why I'm constantly checking your performance, as it's part of my job. Okay, so your job is to report this incident ASAP.

**Peter:** I'll do that right away, Bobby.
